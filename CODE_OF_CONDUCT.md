# Code of Conduct

Data Scope follows the spirit of the [Contributor Covenant](https://www.contributor-covenant.org/version/2/1/code_of_conduct/), version 2.1.

## Our pledge

We want everyone to be able to take part, whatever their experience, background or identity. We act and speak in ways that keep this a welcoming, respectful and harassment-free community.

## What we expect

- Be kind and patient, and assume good intent.
- Give and take feedback on the work, not the person.
- Accept responsibility for mistakes and learn from them.
- Respect people's privacy: never post someone's personal data, or real data from any organisation.

## What is not acceptable

- Harassment, insults, or discriminatory jokes and language.
- Sexual language or imagery, or unwelcome attention.
- Threats, personal attacks or trolling.
- Publishing others' private information without their permission.

## Enforcement

Report problems privately to the maintainers through the contact page at [opensourcecybersecurity.com](https://opensourcecybersecurity.com). Reports are handled confidentially. Maintainers may remove comments, commits, issues and other contributions that break this code, and may ban people who repeatedly or seriously break it.

This applies in all project spaces, and when someone represents the project in public.
